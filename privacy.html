MysteryFate

About & Privacy · last updated 25 November 2026

About

MysteryFate is a free, interactive "what would you do?" story game. Pick a genre, pick a story and make the choices. Some are timed, some are deadly, and every story hides several endings, including a secret one.

It runs entirely in your browser. You can install it as an app (⚙ Settings → Install app, or your browser's "Add to Home Screen"), and after your first visit it also works offline.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies. We don't collect anything about you.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Settings, discovered endings, story maps, saved runs and your journal are kept in your browser's local storage. They are never uploaded. Clear them any time in ⚙ Settings (Journal → Clear, Progress → Reset) or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Offline copy. To work offline, the game's own files (stories, pictures, fonts) are saved in your browser's cache. This contains no personal data.
  • Read-aloud voices use the voices built into your device and browser. Some browsers' online voices (for example Chrome's "Google" voices) send the text being read to the browser maker's speech service. Turn read-aloud off (🗣️ button or ⚙ Settings) if you prefer.
  • Hosting. The game is served by GitHub Pages and also published on itch.io. Like any web host, they may log technical request data such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the itch.io Privacy Policy. The game itself loads nothing from any other website.
  • Children. Some stories contain scary or mature themes and show a content note before you start.
